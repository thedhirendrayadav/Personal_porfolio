04 / PUBLISHING STANDARD

What a useful field note should contain.

Writing is treated as part of the verification process: a way to expose reasoning, supporting material, uncertainty, and the decision that follows.

01QUESTIONDefine the decision or failure being investigated.
02EVIDENCEShow observations, sources, tests, or reproducible behavior.
03LIMITSState what the material cannot establish.
04OPERATIONAL TAKEAWAYTranslate the finding into a decision, control, or next test.
